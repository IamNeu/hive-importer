## Template import specifically
-
- Signup requires email verification and a round trip to the inbox before you
  can reach the dashboard. No Google OAuth option. For a free trial where the
  goal is getting someone into the product fast, that break in the flow is a
  place people drop off. Spectora let me straight in.
- Completing the "Tell us about your business" form did not move the
  "Finish setting up your account" progress (still 4 of 6). The form says it
  helps tailor setup and import, but nothing visibly changed afterwards, so
  there is no signal the answers were used.
- The setup checklist reads 4 of 6 done on a brand new account, before the
  user has done anything. Unclear what the 4 completed steps were.
- The template overview shows counts up front (4 sections, 8 subsections,
  25 fields). Good trust signal — worth having after an import too, so the
  user can immediately check the numbers match what they expected.
- Hive's vocabulary (sections / subsections / fields) differs from Spectora's
  (sections / items / comments). Any importer is translating between two
  vocabularies; worth surfacing that mapping to the user during import.
- Template list to the left, editing pane to the right. Simpler than
  Spectora's three-column layout and easier to follow.
- Editing model: tree on the left, detail pane on the right. Section Title is
  a plain input, Section Description is rich text, plus Private Notes and a
  visibility toggle. Clean separation of plain-text names from rich content.
- No visible Save button — appears to autosave. Nothing on screen confirms a
  change was stored, so the user has to trust it silently worked.
